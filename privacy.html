Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Fabrika Analytics uses. The tool is used only by its owner, Anton Panasenko.

Data we access

  • Read-only data of the owner's own YouTube channel: channel and video details and public statistics (scope youtube.readonly).
  • Read-only YouTube Analytics reports of that channel: views, watch time, subscribers, traffic sources, audience (scope yt-analytics.readonly) and revenue reports (scope yt-analytics-monetary.readonly).

The tool cannot upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.

How data is stored

The Google access token and the reports are stored only on the owner's own computer in files readable only by the owner (the computer's disk is encrypted). No data is stored on servers of this website. This website only hosts these pages, it has no cookies, analytics or advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data received from Google APIs is used only to show the owner his own channel statistics.

Fabrika Analytics' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

Remove the tool's access in your Google account (Security → Third-party apps and services). The local token and reports are then deleted by the owner. To request deletion or ask a question, write to anvitrader@gmail.com.
