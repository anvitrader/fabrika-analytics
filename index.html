Fabrika Analytics

AboutPrivacy Policy

Fabrika Analytics is a personal analytics tool of Anton Panasenko, a content creator. It shows the owner the statistics of his own YouTube channel in his private content dashboard running on his own computer.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in once with his Google account and approves read-only access to his YouTube channel data.
  2. The dashboard requests his channel and video statistics from the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API: views, watch time, subscribers gained and lost, traffic sources and audience reports.
  3. The numbers are shown as charts and lists only to the owner, on his own computer.

Who can use it

Only the owner. The tool is not offered to the public, it does not access any other YouTube channels and it never changes anything on YouTube: all permissions are read-only.
